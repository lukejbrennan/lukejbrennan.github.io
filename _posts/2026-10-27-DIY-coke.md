### 3 absolutely random things from me

I.

I was trying to walk past a couple on a hike recently, and they were chatting to each other but the husband wasn’t really listening but looking back at me, trying to say, honey you’re about to bump into this guy, you gotta stay in your lane...

And when she noticed me (just trying to sliiiide by, no worries!) I pretended to be agreeing with her, listening to her, saying, “no it’s a good point honestly, we should be listening, we should,” joking clearly, and they laughed, and none of us knew at all what the other were talking about because they didn’t speak english and I didn’t speak what they were speaking.
