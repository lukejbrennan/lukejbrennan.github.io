I think sometime in July, Claude came out with a model called Fable 5 and gave everyone limited credits it. Also - I feel like AI is a pretty… idk divisive topic, and I’m conflicted about it for sure. Just want to put that out there. I’m also conflicted about whether or not picking up trash helps the environment, too. Aren’t we just organizing it…? Moving it around?? Shouldn’t we try to make less of it? Shouldn’t we stop making pens??? Don’t we have enough pens???

Anyway, yes, AI, bad and good — personally, as someone who writes code for a living (or used to, sheesh) it’s been kind of incredible to experience. When Fable came out, I saw all these posts on Instagram about how you should use it to make an app, make a clone of minecraft, … how you could make all of these things.

So I tried it. I made a budgeting app like Monarch, a website to help me learn a Scottish accent, something that could post to instagram from the command line, and this nerdy tool that let’s me [traverse my zettlekasten](https://www.lukejbrennan.com/process%20notes/2026/05/21/what-the-freak-is-a-zettlekasten.html). I put it on [github](https://github.com/lukejbrennan/zettel-nav) if anyone wants to… traverse their zettlekasten…

It was crazy! All of these projects that would have previously taken months, or literally years to build came to life in like… an hour. I felt like the bad guy in [Good Will Hunting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PKL2uB1gNK0), who’s sort of obsessed with Matt Damon and fascinated by the fact that he’s just blowing through problems. But also sort of bummed out by the fact that he… exists. So yes, very cool, and also slightly depressing, and also will I have a job, and what about the water??

Anyway, here’s what the zettlekasten tool looks like. I love it!

### Project Updates

**Murphy bed** - Yeahhhh this one, we just gotta… hit real quick. I tried to build a murphy bed in our guest room. But it didn’t fit. Which… I found out at the very end, after we’d already… built it. Total bummer. At one point, we had two murphy beds in our apartment. It was a whole thing. Regardless… it’s over now, thank God. The murphy bed remains an enigma and dream.

**You’re Going to Shit Yourself -** For like… 8 days I’ve been going around giving out my book, _[You’re Going to Shit Yourself](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GZ5YB5RS?lv=shuf&language=es&channelId=500&plpRedirect=mhFallback)_ to strangers on the open road, without context. Results were [mixed](https://www.lukejbrennan.com/30%20days%20of%20marketing%20you're%20going%20to%20shit%20yourself/2026/08/08/day-2-of-marketing-ygtsy.html). The lows were pretty low, but there were some people who laughed, which I’ll take!

**Reacher Grabber!?** - David Sedaris mentioned in an interview that he goes on walks and picks up trash, maybe as a way to think and/or to think of what to write. I’m not 100p sure, but that was the sense that I got. Anyway, I became obsessed with that idea, so I bought a reacher grabber on amazon (just a trash picker-upper) and I’ve been taking it out! Hitting the town! So far so good, and I’m excited for this as a part-volunteering, part-writing exercise. But also… is picking up trash good? Probably, but you’ll need to sell me on it. But I’m open to that, which is to say, I’m listening, and also send help.
