III.

[Scientists at the University of Michigan](http://ns.umich.edu/new/releases/8167) developed a mathematical model allowing a super-high-energy electron laser to rip apart nothingness (a vacuum) into its matter and antimatter components. 

Basically, what we call “nothing” is actually a perfect balance of positive and negative components – our universe could essentially just be an expanding “nothingness” caused by quantum fluctuation.

{% include linebreak.html %}

IV.

"If you start getting bored, that is an excellent sign that your meditation is progressing. OK, sit through the hot boredom until it becomes cool boredom.

Hot boredom is what we’re familiar with — jumpiness. You want to get out of there.

Boredom has this quality of just wanting to bolt. And cool boredom is: You sit there with a feeling of boredom."

-- From [This Is Why I Find Pema Chödrön So Essential](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/15/opinion/ezra-klein-podcast-pema-chodron.html), Ezra Klein's Interview with the GOAT

{% include linebreak.html %}

V. 

I'm constantly looking for an excuse to slow down. Once in an interview, I said I was a slow problem solver, and the guy was like... ehhh let's go with "deliberate" rather than "slow", right? 

But I like slow.

Here's an essay on reading [From Ryan Holiday](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdwQJJkFkQ1/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&lqvp=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==). A snippet: "To be a great reader, you spend a lot of time doing it -- slowly, patiently, attentively, without trying to make it go faster, without being in any hurry."

{% include linebreak.html %}

VI. 

"Trying to control everything is a symptom of anxiety... 'if you have everything under control, what's there to worry about?'"

From a book that I picked up initially because the title made me laugh: [13 Things Mentally Strong People Don't Do](https://amymorinlcsw.com/mentally-strong-people/) by Amy Morin, LCSW. I imagined funny versions of things that mentally strong people don't do... like sit in bean bag chairs. 

Anyway, I read the first chapter and realized that this list was initially a note that Morin wrote herself, on a piece of paper at her own kitchen table. A last ditch effort to get a grip on her life as it was quickly falling apart around her.
