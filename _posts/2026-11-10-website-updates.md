The [Holy See website](https://www.vatican.va/content/vatican/en.html) kind of slaps. 

One thing that I was admiring about it was the simplicity, and also the ease by which you can click around and link to things. In the Magnifica Humanitas, you can click to different sections by adding a #section-name-here to the eend of the url. I really liked that, so I added it!

Now you can go to a bible verse (or any section title!) by adding the section name. For example... to go to the 5th verse in john chapter two, you can go... 

https://www.lukejbrennan.com/notes/jn-02/#v5

I added a page for [recipes](https://www.lukejbrennan.com/recipes/)! It's so simple... literally just a list of recipes with links to where they came from... maybe I'll update it with pictures, or filters of some kind in the future, but they're up!

Search works across the website. From any page, just type "/" to get the search going. It matches words within all posts. 

That's it! not as much as I'd thought, lol, but alas. These are some updates!
