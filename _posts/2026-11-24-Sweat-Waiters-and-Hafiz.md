### 3 absolutely random things from me

I.

Like... 4 years ago I found a study that said that humans can tell the difference between sweat that was caused by workouts and sweat that was caused by ... anticipating skydiving (aka... doing something fearful!)

In searching for that study, I found [this other study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7209933/), which "brings new evidence to show that fear intensity is encoded in sweat." So not only can you "categorically" tell what emotion someone is having in sweat... but sweat also lets you know how scary the scary thing is.

Crazy!

III.

Reading  [The Difference Between](https://www.henribruning.nl/theo/boeken/Hafiz/blz102E.htm), I kept coming back to the imagery of Yasamin, and how to me, today, she seems to represent the poor and unhoused:

"She had not combed her hair or bathed for months;

IV. 

I've never had my bullshit called out faster than when I was working at homeless resource center. 

I was trying to explain to this guy that... if he had a job interview, a doctor's appointment, etc, then! he could have a bus pass. But unless he had (proof of...) one of those, then I couldn't help him. 

I made it through maybe 1 second of that spiel before the guy said: "Look man if you're not going to help me just say so."

{% include linebreak.html %}

V. 

I think the poor / unhoused are some of the most adept at smelling the truth on people / seeing through bs.

{% include linebreak.html %}

VI. 

Going the other way? Waiters are the people that are the least smelly. It's hard for me to figure out what they're actually going through.

Sometimes they let on, but more often than not I have no idea. Happy or not happy, idk. Do they love us or is this all an act???
