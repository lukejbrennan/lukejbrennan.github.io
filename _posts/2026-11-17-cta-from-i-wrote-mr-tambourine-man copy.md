II.

[Robert Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Greene_(American_author)) has a [clip on instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/CuFHmOsg-PV/?xtok=bjZnNzR6YmxoMGhp) that I consistently think about. I went back and watched it, and it's a little different than I remembered. Here's the text of the original:

"The key to the ability to transform ourselves is actually insanely simple. To reverse this perspective, stop fixating on what other people are saying and doing, on the money, the connections, the outward appearance of things. Instead, look inward. Focus on the smaller internal changes that lay the groundwork for a much larger change in fortune."

Sure. I buy it! Very Atomic Habits. 

The first time I listened to this, I heard... "look for the small internal changes that are *already happening*." 

Rather than focusing on the things that you could be doing, assume that you are sort of... moving in the direction that you want to be. You're doing the things that the best version of yourself *would be doing*. 

The changes aren't massive! These are "smaller internal changes." And maybe they're only visible to you, but they're happening! What are *those* things? What are the ways that you're *already* choosing to be that person?
