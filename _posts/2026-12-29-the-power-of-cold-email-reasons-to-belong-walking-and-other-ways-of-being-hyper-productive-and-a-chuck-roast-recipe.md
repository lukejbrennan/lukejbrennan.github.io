### 3 absolutely random things from me

I.

There's an artist supply store (Blick) near my house, and like going in to bookstores, or the post office, it has a good smell. A paper smell? I like being in the store for some reason, and I'm not exactly sure why. Just looking around in a bookstore or in Blick feels luxurious. The things I *could* buy. It has a  getting-ready-for-school feeling. 

I picked up the most luxurious notebook i could find (big paper, no lines), and bought it, and the cashier asked if I had an account with Blick, if I was a member, and I'm trying to do more of what the world asks me to do, so I said, I didn't, and they asked, would you like one? (But listen, it was the end of the shift, they were about to close, really. The staff was already chatting and debriefing, starting to put things in place for tomorrow), so there was also a voice saying I shouldn't say yes. *Don't be a bother.* But I did anyway, and now every time I go back I think: *I'm a freaking member. A member along with all these other members. All these other painters, mostly.*

II.

Yesterday, walking across a field, I thought: this could be a battlefield. I could be in a war right now, marching my way toward my hand getting ripped off by a cannonball or something. But instead, I'm just holding a coffee, and honestly, it's no big deal if "work's been kinda busy lately" because, like, I'm not going to war right now. 

And that's something to be grateful for.
