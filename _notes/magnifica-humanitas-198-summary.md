Talk of "artificial moral agents" is wrong — moral judgment requires conscience, personal responsibility, and recognizing the other as a person. Lethal or irreversible decisions cannot be delegated to machines. No algorithm makes war morally acceptable. AI only makes conflict faster and more impersonal, normalizing violence as something to be optimized rather than prevented.
