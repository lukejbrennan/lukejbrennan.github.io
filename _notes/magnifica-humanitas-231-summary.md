At the heart of everything is the Incarnation. The vulnerable flesh of the Son reflects the flesh of brothers and sisters stripped of dignity. In the Lord's closeness, we find the true humanity God calls us to: openness, communion, letting ourselves be moved by the suffering of the little ones.
