# Enchiridion XXVII
*Epictetus, tr. T. W. Higginson (1865)*

[← 26](/notes/enchiridion-26/) | [Enchiridion](/notes/enchiridion/) | [28 →](/notes/enchiridion-28/)

---

As a mark[^1] is not set up for the sake of missing the aim, so neither does the nature of evil exist in the world.

---

[^1]: Happiness, the effect of virtue, is the mark which God has set up for us to aim at. Our missing it is no work of His; nor so properly anything real, as a mere negative and failure of our own.
