Colonialism returns in new forms: now it takes data. Health records, genetic maps, demographic profiles — these are the new rare earths. Those who control the health data of whole populations gain structural leverage to shape markets, guide investments, and decide who matters. Data must become a true common good, with people retaining the right to control how it is used.
