from [joy in being the driver](/notes/joy-in-being-the-driver/) and living a dream someone else has made for us

We live in contexts that most often were created organically, by the powers that be, or by powers that wanted us to be some sort of way. Some contexts were created by the great arc of history and by traditions, and by the generations of people going about the world living a certain way. We are living in contexts created by our history and our culture. 

Some contexts are created with intention - structures of power that benefit the powerful. 

We live out of all these different scripts, and choose the lines in them that fit the appropriate context. We are constantly context switching. 

Sometimes we can choose a context, which is powerful. 

We can also create entirely new contexts, which is also powerful. I think we are rarely in the driver seat, consciously creating or participating actively in creating a context. Creating context worth living in.
