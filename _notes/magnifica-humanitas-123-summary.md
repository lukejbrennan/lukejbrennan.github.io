History also shows humanity's remarkable capacity to build institutions that protect human life: the Red Cross (1863), abolition of slavery, the UN and Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948), the Refugee Convention (1951). These achievements were hard-won and remain fragile — but they show that moral progress is possible.
