Cyberspace is a new battleground. Cyberattacks and AI-orchestrated influence campaigns can destabilize countries without open warfare. Attribution is often uncertain, raising the risk of escalation. Diplomacy must negotiate shared rules for the digital domain to protect civilians from invisible but real forms of violence.
