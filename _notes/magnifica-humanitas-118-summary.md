We are in a cultural crisis about weakness. Everything that limits us — illness, old age, suffering, vulnerability — is seen as a defect to fix. But the Christian tradition teaches that humanity often flourishes *through* limitations, not despite them. Finitude isn't a problem to solve; it's the ground on which we learn compassion and encounter God.
