Three criteria for AI in war: (1) personal responsibility — the chain of accountability must be identifiable; (2) moral timeframe — speed must never be the supreme value in decisions about life and death; (3) protection of civilians — technologies that allow attacking without seeing human faces lower the moral threshold of conflict.
