There's also a hidden story: religious communities serving in dangerous places, everyday martyrs of ordinary life — parents, nurses, teachers, volunteers who accompany the lonely and the outcast without fanfare. Goodness doesn't advance automatically; it requires perseverance and willingness to begin again after defeat.
