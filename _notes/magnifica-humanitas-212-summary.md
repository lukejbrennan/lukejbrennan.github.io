It's tempting to think: the problems are too big, my choices don't matter. This is resignation dressed as realism. No one is without responsibility. Each of us, in our own sphere, must choose between fueling the mentality of force (through indifference, lies, hatred) or preserving the mindset of peace (through truth, moderation, closeness, care).
