We need to "disarm" AI — free it from the logic of armed competition (geopolitical, economic, cognitive). This means rejecting the idea that technical power equals the right to govern. It means making AI human-friendly, open to debate, and accessible to all cultures. AI is already our environment — we must make it welcoming, not hostile.
