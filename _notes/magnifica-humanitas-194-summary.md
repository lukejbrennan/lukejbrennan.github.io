Nuclear arsenals are growing again, not shrinking. The Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (2021) is an important step but largely symbolic since nuclear powers haven't signed it. The belief that nuclear deterrence is necessary for security is widespread but wrong, and it's driving a new arms race.
