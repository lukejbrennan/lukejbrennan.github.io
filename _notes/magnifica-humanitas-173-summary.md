Nothing about AI is immaterial. Behind every seamless AI response is a chain of exploitation: millions of poorly paid workers labeling data and moderating disturbing content; children in dangerous conditions mining the rare earths that power our devices; criminal networks using digital infrastructure to traffic human beings. Technology that promises emancipation while producing hidden subordination contradicts human dignity.
