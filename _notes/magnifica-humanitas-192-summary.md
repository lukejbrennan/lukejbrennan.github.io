The media and digital dimensions add propaganda, fake news, and friend-or-foe narratives to the mix. The "just war" theory, already often misused to justify any war, is now outdated. Humanity has far better tools: dialogue, diplomacy, forgiveness. Violence always leaves catastrophic consequences for civilians.
