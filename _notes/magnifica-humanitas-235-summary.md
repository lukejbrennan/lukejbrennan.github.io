The "Amen" we say at the Eucharist shapes our whole lives. We are the Body of Christ — and that means the Eucharist opens us to justice, sharing, and preferential care for those burdened by poverty. When digital networks generate exclusion and isolation, the Church nourished by the Eucharist offers a different model.
