Digital technology is changing war: cyberattacks, information manipulation, and automated strategic decisions are now part of conflict. AI lowers the threshold for using force, shields people from responsibility, and reduces the enemy to a statistic. Social Doctrine's principles must guide our judgment about these technologies too.
