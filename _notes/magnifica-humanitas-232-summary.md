Transhumanism promises an enhanced humanity beyond limitation — but the Incarnation opens a completely different path. God doesn't eliminate our weakness — He enters it. The living God descends into our history to free us, transforming our limitations into spaces of salvation. What saves humanity is divine love entering the most fragile point of our history.
