The Church grew too slowly in condemning slavery — past popes even regulated and legitimized forms of it. Only in the 19th century was slavery fully and clearly condemned. This is a wound in Christian memory. The delay is not excused — though past events can't be judged anachronistically. For the immense suffering caused, I sincerely ask for pardon.
