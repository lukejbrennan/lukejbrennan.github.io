The image of Jerusalem rebuilt points forward to the New Jerusalem of Revelation — a gift descending from God, a city with open gates for all nations. Its walls are not defensive but adorning; its living water is offered freely; its tree of life heals the nations. This vision encourages us to overcome divisions and work together.
