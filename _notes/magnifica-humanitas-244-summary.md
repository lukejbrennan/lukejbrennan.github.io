Mary teaches us to see the world from below: through the eyes of those who suffer, not the mighty; through the perspective of the widow, the orphan, the wounded child, the exile. She is the poet and prophetess of Redemption, proclaiming the *Magnificat* — the most innovative hymn ever sung.
