We live in a time of spiritual and cultural blindness. False pragmatism severs the roots of history. The dynamics that produced the 20th century's atrocities are re-emerging under new guises — hybrid wars, disinformation, manufactured fear. Meanwhile, the poorest pay the price as military spending crowds out healthcare and education.
