Interreligious dialogue is crucial to peace. The great spiritual traditions carry a message of peace at their core. Anyone who uses religion to justify terrorism or war betrays religion itself. The "spirit of Assisi" — believers drawing on the deepest sources of their traditions — has no room for "sanctified hatred."
