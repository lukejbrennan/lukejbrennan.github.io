# Letter CXXIV — On the True Good as Attained by Reason
*Seneca, tr. Richard M. Gummere (1917–25)*

[← Letter 123](/notes/seneca-123-on-the-conflict-between-pleasure-and-virtue/) | [Moral Letters](/notes/moral-letters/)

---

Full many an ancient precept could I give,

Didst thou not shrink, and feel it shame to learn

Such lowly duties.[^1071]

---

[^1071]: Virgil, *Georgics* I. 176 f.
